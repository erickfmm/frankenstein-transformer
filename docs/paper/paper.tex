\begin{abstract}
The transformer design space has fragmented: attention spans recurrent, sparse, gated, latent, memory-augmented and fast-weight blocks, and optimizers extend beyond AdamW, so consistent comparison remains costly. We present Frankenstein Transformer, a configuration-driven CLI toolkit that unifies model definition, training, deployment and inference behind one strict YAML schema: 56 sequence mixers in nine families, 39 optimizers with per-parameter-group control, and encoder, decoder, Vision Transformer and audio classes covering language, vision and speech tasks. Its contribution is a validated experimentation contract: invalid configurations fail before training starts, every component shares one interface, and one document drives training, quantized export and a schema-generated web builder. Measurement rounds exercise the contract; the largest trains 1,653 configurations (3,668 CPU-hours), covering the complete $54\times54$ mixer atlas, a 39-optimizer sweep and 168 ablations: the mixer stack dominates the design space, and training loss barely predicts held-out quality. All rounds ship as interactive reports with downloadable datasets.
\end{abstract}
